# Architecture and authority

TypeScript + Canvas 2D + semantic DOM terminal UI; Vite builds the local browser app. A small Node HTTP relay keeps optional bridge credentials server-side and never executes learner code. This avoids a heavyweight engine boundary between a responsive code editor and real-time world simulation.

- `model.ts`: serializable state and configurable defaults. Deterministic seeded RNG; simulation and evidence sequences are distinct from wall-clock retention dates.
- `world.ts`: frozen seeded floors, one-use encounter/station identity and finite furniture/loot; 20 survivors and four building floors plus roof.
- `spatial.ts`: geometry, collision, ray/LOS and weighted path search. Zombies seek open alternatives before breaking furniture. Furniture objects retain independent HP and footprints.
- `navigation.ts`: actor-width grid/portal graph with explicit door and complete vault traversals; cached by actual floor geometry. Door approaches and vault directions/landings are planned together, with collision checks through the entire trajectory.
- `npc-threat-memory.ts`: bounded copies of actual local sightings for retreat planning, separate from current perception/rendering. Six-second/floor limits, nearest-eight capacity, sampled positions and immediate new sightings preserve uncertainty without following hidden actors.
- `zombie-stairs.ts`: pure completed-flight arrival decisions, static/furniture/body collision, protective fields and one oldest-flight contact at the actual upper exit. Shared engine strike handlers retain ordinary damage/cooldown/Surge behavior.
- `stair-travel.ts`: persisted survivor departure/destination and fixed two-second flight; personal access at entry, source vulnerability, movement cancellation, connected clear landing and blocked-arrival queueing. Both human and NPC traversal use the same state machine.
- `engine.ts`: authoritative 30Hz world step, AI utilities/cognition, contact damage, personal stairs, extraction and Surge. No model calls in physics. NPC-only world outcomes are explicitly simulated and never human evidence.
- `npc-outcome.ts`: explicit on-demand reconstruction of committed simulated NPC-only completions. Frozen milestones and contributor profiles bind a retained candidate, separate semantic validation, exact retry and first-view exposure. No retroactive edits, world reward changes, invented runtime execution or learner evidence.
- `ai.ts`: immutable task issuance, versioned workspace/submissions, free tutoring adapter, grounded dialogue and separate post-completion assessment proposals. Development mode is labeled everywhere. Accepted submission snapshots freeze their own revision.
- `curriculum.ts`: source projection and separate development/live study views. Development visits never allocate formal learner identifiers or alter academic cells.
- `run-learning.ts`: shared death/fresh-run preservation of actual human context, including displayed but unanswered tasks; archives exact questions/drafts and interrupts unavailable encounter bindings.
- `learner.ts` / `learning-model.ts`: source-bound live ledger, stable generated facet definitions, scoped assessment transactions, immutable receipts, formal session/attempt/evidence IDs, bounded visit rotation, review timing, lower-gate reopening, suspended higher positions and curriculum-only continuations. Whole checkpoints need complete independently evidenced facet coverage and a separate scope-coverage review; a model pass flag alone cannot change the denominator.
- `persistence.ts` / `storage.ts`: ordered IndexedDB transactions with checksummed snapshots and monotonic checkpoint ordering, plus a small synchronous recovery copy. Legacy localStorage saves migrate on read. Large archives are not limited to localStorage capacity. Exact world/RNG restoration, JSON backup and pending-request recovery preserve new-run/old-encounter separation.
- `roadmap-identifiers.ts`: shared high-water counters for runtime allocation, parsing, export and adoption. Actual progress registries and explicit reservations set lower bounds; arbitrary prose and next-ID predictions do not issue tasks. S0.7 carries per-session attempt/evidence floors and global session/gap/decision floors. Adoption cannot lower known counters. Unknown legacy counter claims require reconciliation.
- `roadmap-export.ts`: complete source-preserving semantic Markdown projection, per-facet proof and gaps, shared IDs/history and actual-parent SHA-256 revision ancestry. Prepared exports commit to the game only after integrity checks. The persisted cache makes unchanged redownloads byte-identical and prevents preview-only revisions. Raw task/workspace/chat/assessment rationales never enter the roadmap.
- `workspace-history.ts`: exact revision reconstruction from attributed editor deltas; full drafts and accepted submissions are stored separately, avoiding quadratic keystroke history growth.
- `render.ts` / `audio.ts` / `style.css`: finite 360-degree player vision blocked only by walls, dynamic wall-clipped darkness, procedural art, downloaded SFX and procedural ambience/alerts, gap-aware muffling and breach cues; separate full Surge palette across the world and every study surface.
- `knowledge-probe.ts`: the Terminal AI probing stance and its validated reply contract (`outcome`, `knowledge.facets` with implied/declared sub-facets, answer signals). Per-encounter probe state, carried `study.knowledgeEstablished` and `study.knowledgeGaps` records (closed when a facet is later established), the next-request context and the generator's `probeRecords`. Terminal observations only; never ledger mastery.
- `topic-spawn.ts`: which topic a terminal spawns. The scheduler's formal visit stays reachable; other terminals rotate across the tier's unfinished topics with a cooldown, cooled gap topics first, honouring player-disabled topics. Variety terminals carry a `practice` contract that never binds a visit, creates an attempt or is assessed.
- `inspector-view.ts`: pure HTML builders for the curriculum inspector (summary, readiness, established facets and gaps, topic variety). `main.ts` owns the DOM, handlers and persistence.
- `scripts/import-curriculum.mjs`: validates original bytes and protected digests before making the runtime import. Any source update requires an explicit import/migration rather than silently trusting stale metadata.
- `scripts/terminal-relay-app.mjs` / `scripts/terminal-mcp-app.mjs`: the live terminal relay and the synthetic demo as MCP Apps, sharing `scripts/lib/mcp-app.mjs` (tool schema, OAuth, UI resource and argument checks) and `scripts/lib/probe-reply.mjs` (the transport-side probe reply schema and checks, also used by the handoff queue). `scripts/lib/diagnostic-state.mjs` holds the owner-only JSON state used by the ChatGPT smoke and diagnostic scripts.

## Trust boundaries and limitations

Chat/NPC/problem strings are escaped or assigned through textContent. Structured completion and evidence have scoped validators. The bridge relay accepts only known operation names and bounded JSON requests; binds loopback; tokens stay in environment. The bridge transport does not evaluate submitted code in the relay process. The separate `/api/execute` route delegates to disposable Docker containers with no network or host mounts, a non-root UID, a read-only root filesystem, dropped capabilities/no new privileges, bounded tmpfs, memory/CPU/PIDs/output and wall time. No host-execution fallback exists.

The client is authoritative for a local single-player game, not an adversarial multiplayer service. Browser diagnostics permit inspection during development and are not anti-cheat. Import is intended for the user's own exported saves; schema validation currently checks integrity and core structure, not every nested field. Do not import untrusted third-party game saves.

Current ledger handling protects evidence by refusing unsupported promotion. This means live full curriculum progress is incomplete, not that a frozen D1 practice loop satisfies the final specification. Identifier allocation and live ledger transactions are implemented and fixture-verified. The merged-file export transaction is implemented and tested with synthetic learning fixtures plus actual browser downloads/reload. Importing a subsequently edited external Markdown revision still requires a separate source migration/reconciliation workflow; a game JSON backup already resumes the full ledger and raw artifacts.

Human editor evidence stores both revision snapshots and actual inserted/removed text, preserving the distinction between supplied starter code and player edits. Task/starter exposure is recorded once when first presented, separately from generation and fictional NPC event times. The assessor receives these sources, actual human artifacts, private help, the accepted submission and authoritative track criteria. A changed or missing supplied-context attribution cannot certify independent work.

Encounter references include a UUID run identity; location names reused in a new building do not collide with old study visits. Partial learning and raw artifacts survive death in archives, while new locks start with new workspaces. Pending assessments can be retried with their original evidence cutoff; interrupted live requests never substitute development completions.


Physical contact now reserves face-specific attack positions throughout each attacker's cooldown. Rear queues cannot reach through a front body, and a narrow end face cannot inherit the object's unrelated long-side width. New outside arrivals use empty physical slots; legacy overlaps recover through bounded legal movement. The entrance closure waits for occupants rather than creating a collider through a body. The initial closure window is three seconds in new runs, ahead of the ordinary outside wave; existing saved object state remains intact.

Furniture captures its initial placement/orientation as part of the run. Defense qualification compares actual approach connectivity against that initial state, with an existing closed door treated open for the comparison so it cannot hide a second layer. Late touching or returning initially sealing furniture to its old pose earns no new contribution; current physical helpers can share a decisive placement. No durability/resistance bonus is added. Strategy and long-study pacing remain unaccepted.


## Physical study-space and congestion audit

New normal floors cycle through three seeded hall families. Each includes one narrow inner study annex, accessible terminals, staged finite furniture and one corridor fridge. Preparation is physical: footprints are checked against complete tile rectangles, slight alignment correction consumes ordinary movement time, and an object integrates only once per world tick even when multiple handlers participate. A handler must be able to follow its own pull. New-run shelves use 3,200 HP; saved furniture is not upgraded on load.

Navigation caches exact route computations against floor tiles and all relevant furniture geometry/open/destroyed state; returned routes are independent mutable copies. Zombies skip nearby route guides only with a swept clear segment, align before a narrow opening, and yield locally to a closer queue member when blocked. Recovery uses bounded normal movement and collision checks. A sanitized captured 90-zombie doorway jam is a regression fixture; no learner history is present. Contact slots are additionally limited by the actually exposed world tile along the attack face, so a broad shelf behind a one-cell mouth cannot receive attacks from inaccessible neighboring positions.

Vaulting retains the traversed object identity. Attack sight ignores only that object while the survivor is raised, preventing an invulnerability interval; other geometry still occludes. Occupied zombie landings are rejected. NPC thought progresses at reduced rate during vaulting, and a survivor already beside an available pickup boards before considering flight from nearby danger.

The following camera fits the entire finite sight circle at a legible scale. Higher-floor entry stairs are labeled RETURN when visible; existing physical descent remains available. The defense lab records continuous attack pressure as well as survival, because congestion-induced inactivity is not a credible defensive success.


`execution.ts` captures a complete workspace revision before dispatch. Edits can continue; the asynchronous receipt is accepted only for the captured code hash/language and immutable image ID. Its history entry refers to the actual contributing edit sequences and the stored job. The assessor receives real output as untrusted text; the learning validator requires that stored receipt rather than accepting a free-form execution claim. No automatic task completion or academic credit is coupled to running. Pending jobs recover as interrupted after reload, in both active and archived encounters. Archives keep the original encounter object so a late result can arrive after a fresh run without appearing in the new lock.

`scripts/code-runner.mjs` resolves a trusted installed image, creates a stopped restricted container, then attaches input to a separately started container. Cleanup targets only that generated container name. A container-side timeout plus host-side bounds contain runaway jobs; startup failure is not reported as execution. The interpreter emits a private per-job startup marker; only a complete marker line counts, so a Docker/bootstrap diagnostic containing command source cannot impersonate a successful start. Output decoding is bounded even for invalid UTF-8. `test:runner` performs actual container and HTTP pipeline tests, independently of the fast engine suite. The optional container browser workflow uses a synthetic test-mode curriculum with real execution; no human learning is claimed.


## Surface grips, staged fortification and exit recovery

`handling.ts` checks actual surface reach, body clearance, wall rays and exact intersections with intervening furniture. A nearby object hidden behind a door cannot steal a reachable grab. Movement and rotation reject living zombies in the resulting footprint. Active physical helpers share one bounded translation; every handler must be able to follow that translation, and a blocked handler stops it. Helpers across a wall/door supply no speed or defense credit. New position integration remains once per object per tick.

`fortification.ts` plans real object transport with the full footprint and a feasible handler sweep. It first tries two cardinal legs, then a bounded half-tile search with exact target axes. This creates a sequence of physical handling positions rather than dragging diagonally into a desk or wall. NPC plans persist in actor state and stop/reconsider after failed progress. Being moved by another handler earlier in the same tick counts as progress. Object HP and collision dimensions never change as a planning shortcut.

NPCs finish their planned placement even if a contribution qualifies before the final pose. If a closed door prevents a legal vault out of a sealed room, an NPC physically moves the cover inward before attempting the exit again. This applies to newcomers without a credential as well as contributors; it does not grant or borrow stair access. Active exit handling retains its navigation target while the plan runs and cannot recursively request another exit from inside its own handling controller.

Exploration retains a destination until arrival. Previously the fallback recomputed the opposite hall end from the actor's current Y each frame, producing a permanent reversal at the midline. Failed destinations are remembered briefly as unreachable, rather than falsely recorded as visited. `navigation.ts` now checks continuous expanded actor footprints against walls and furniture; sampled shallow corner intersections had admitted unusable route edges.

The NPC benchmark supports `SIM_SECONDS`, `SIM_STEP`, `SIM_SEEDS`, `SIM_REPORT` and optional `SIM_SNAPSHOT` for a private full diagnostic. Reports identify the timestep/configuration/source fingerprint and retain progression, routes and credentials. Counts accumulate events across the ring buffer. The human is explicitly absent/marked escaped in this NPC-only diagnostic; it awards no academic learning and does not substitute for a human play session.


## Route exposure, evacuation and curriculum context

`survivor-strategy.ts` scores danger along the complete retreat route, rather than only its destination. It considers actual traversable vault paths as well as body-clear walking segments. Vault landings occupied by a living survivor or zombie are rejected using the physical landing rule. Available aircraft add an evacuation bias; new pickup trips invalidate stale flight choices. No survivor speed, healing or extra inventory is supplied by planning.

Station discovery, claiming, continuing the human terminal interface and collecting finite loot require a nearby visible station. Walls, closed doors and opaque cover block use as well as rendering. Opening or destroying the real obstacle restores access. Ordinary low desks still allow eye-level interaction. These checks do not grant stair credentials.

`historical-scope.ts` extracts source-attributed gap records and exact evidence rows for the scheduled objective/depth into live task contracts. Preserved and missing prose, assistance, source hash and minimal next checks remain distinguishable from new machine-verifiable facet proofs. Reading history cannot mint evidence or promote a checkpoint. Depth/track filtering excludes future-only gaps and colliding objective numbers. Later ledger facet proofs remain separate; automatic historical-to-new-facet equivalence and external Markdown reconciliation remain unfinished.

Generated questions are validated against the current scheduled track/objectives/depth, review status, phase and ledger version. After validation their contract is replaced with the local canonical contract so a provider cannot strip history/evidence constraints or rewrite scheduler metadata.


`final-requirements.ts` derives the actual selected final-gate scope from the source transfer/integration/ENV tables. Track-qualified topic IDs refer to transfers without creating ordinary D4 cells. Selected extensions use their explicit objective targets and do not acquire extra transfer gates. Integration definitions retain their authoritative criteria and source scope choices. The inspector reports separate checkpoint, transfer, interview-integration, environment and delayed-retention counts. Supported new checkpoint P states require nonempty reviewed facets and matching independent evidence; historical gap reconciliation remains depth-specific. ENV and retention are separate from the Tier-4 gate. This is a read-only source/ledger readiness layer; final-requirement scheduling, new evidence recording and export projection remain unfinished.

### Run-scoped sound and accessibility

`soundscape.ts` derives a bounded same-floor ambient mix and positioned damage/breach/extraction cues from actual world events. Player acoustic transmission uses `acoustics.ts`: a cached vertical decomposition of the obstacle-polygon union into connected free-space components. Slabs split at every polygon vertex and non-axis-aligned edge intersection. This detects narrow/diagonal gaps and alternate room entrances independently of actor clearance and the coarse walking grid. Latched leaves use their sealed jamb footprint; swung leaves use their actual rotated box. Sealed sources get 0.025 transmission and 120 Hz low-pass; open or indirect paths remain clearer. NPC hearing remains on its existing spatial model. Nearest candidates and strongest contributors bound work and avoid arbitrary spawn-order selection. World events retain optional positions for sound only. Legacy events lacking positions use a quiet same-floor fallback; neither captions nor audio claim precise hidden enemy identities/counts.

`sfx.ts` loads 16 locally bundled CC0 voice/foley assets; `public/sfx/sources.json` records authors, URLs, conversion and hashes. There are no music assets or music channel. `AudioDirector` owns the Web Audio graph, per-voice low-pass filters, gain/compressor and a bounded set of 20 cue voices. Decoded samples replace oscillator impacts; synthetic effects/captions remain available if loading fails. Simulation-paced groans, footsteps and dragging play only with actual nearby sources or player movement. Existing ambience voices crossfade their own gain/filter targets at each acoustic update when geometry changes, rather than retaining their initial unmuffled sound until the sample ends. A new Game object or rewound simulation resets its cursor/tick/phase and cancels existing voices. Phase changes cancel warning voices. Warning sound is scheduled only as short current-phase slices/pulses during updates, without wall-clock timers or future-run callbacks. The current elapsed warning time determines pitch and pulse index on recovery. Run end stops voices and fades the ambient bus. Audio failure does not prevent entering gameplay; captions continue to derive from simulation. Captions update only when their text changes and remain opt-in.

Reduced-motion rendering stabilizes decorative canvas effects as well as CSS transitions. It changes no visibility, movement or curriculum rules. Real Chromium OfflineAudioContext metrics and canvas-pixel/editor-focus checks supplement mocked-node timing and physical-event tests. Perceptual comfort and actual assistive-technology behavior are still external human acceptance work.

### Committed retreat and contact-aware routes

NPC retreat has a three-second maximum commitment scoped to its actual floor and persistent through a save/reload. Reaching the waypoint, an occupied visible endpoint, a floor transition, or an actual boarding opportunity can end/reconsider it. A survivor does not immediately reverse toward work merely because an enemy crossed the original reaction radius for one frame.

A perceived living attacker newly entering contact space along the remaining committed route also triggers reconsideration. Destination clearance alone is insufficient for a moving pack. Selection records the visible threat IDs already accounted for by its physical route; unavoidable known contacts do not reroll a least-bad plan every frame. The bounded timer and endpoint checks remain, and fresh threat IDs can interrupt. This small record persists across reload and is replaced on a new retreat/floor. Reconsideration discards the old physical path/navigation cache even when the replacement destination is within the ordinary follower's0.75-tile tolerance. The replacement still uses actual collision/navigation/vaulting and the bounded retreat interval; it grants no speed, HP, damage or learning advantage. `retreat-cutoff.json` retains a real30HP rooftop survivor under the preceding source, with84 rooftop zombies and an actual active pickup. Replaying that captured state tests dynamic route cutoff rather than merely endpoint occupancy. `ROOF_FIXTURE` and `ROOF_REPORT` let `benchmark:roof` replay this fixture without overwriting the older roof-pressure report.

`routeContacts` distinguishes an approach into perceived contact space from a route that moves away from an initially close enemy. The scorer makes that a strong cost rather than looking only at a clear destination. `unsafeReturn` applies the same check to the actual stair, helipad, remembered task station or retained exploration goal before a retreat releases. It uses the already perception-filtered living enemy set and remembered station IDs. It neither executes academic work remotely nor transfers the original puzzle. Movement/collision/vault timing and all health/damage/speed values remain unchanged. Weapon use refreshes the live threat set before making the decision.

### Remembered task selection and physical seat continuity

`task-strategy.ts` ranks only current-floor remembered eligible stations using actual survivor routes, traversal costs, perceived live enemies and visible occupancy. Consumed locks, foreign solo owners and unreachable targets remain unavailable. Loot competes with mandatory personal access according to role, remaining capacity, health and owned healing. Ranking is deterministic and does not consume world randomness, issue questions or update learning. Existing valid unfinished intentions are retained; unsuccessful searches retry after one simulated second or fresh floor/discovery state.

The engine carries a selected seat separately from a physical station reservation. A new intention clears a stale reservation; builders do not reserve a chair merely by planning. Under perceived danger the follower retains the safer usable seat rather than replacing it with the nearest seat in the same shared encounter. Without perceived danger the established nearest-seat behavior remains. Retreat return checks the retained physical preference, including after reload. Re-ranking every normal seat by route cost was tested and rejected after fewer extractions; its reports retain their original source fingerprint. This policy does not supply hidden enemy information or bypass physical terminal access.

### Making space for physical transport

`furniture-yield.ts` detects a non-handler in the imminent footprint of a visible living teammate's active furniture plan. It considers a small imminent displacement, then seeks a real survivor route to clear the current transport leg, avoiding occupied endpoints and contact with perceived enemies.
